# Papers And Sources Used

## Closest Benchmark

1. Prediction Arena: Benchmarking AI Models on Real-World Prediction Markets.
   - Web: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07355
   - Used for: repeated agent decision design, objective market outcomes, token/time cost framing.

## Stablecoin Price Stability

2. Richard K. Lyons and Ganesh Viswanath-Natraj. "What Keeps Stablecoins Stable?"
   - Used for: stablecoin peg-stability and price-deviation framing.

3. Dirk G. Baur and Lai T. Hoang. "The Stable in Stablecoins." Finance Research Letters.
   - Used for: stablecoins as assets expected to maintain low volatility around the peg.

4. Gary B. Gorton and Jeffery Y. Zhang. "Taming Wildcat Stablecoins."
   - Used for: fragility, run-risk, and trust/regulation framing.

5. European Central Bank. "In search for stability in crypto-assets: are stablecoins the solution?"
   - Used for: institutional framing of stablecoin stability mechanisms and risks.

## Forecast Evaluation And Trustworthiness

6. Glenn W. Brier. "Verification of Forecasts Expressed in Terms of Probability." Monthly Weather Review, 1950.
   - Used for: Brier score.

7. Tilmann Gneiting and Adrian E. Raftery. "Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation." Journal of the American Statistical Association, 2007.
   - Used for: proper probabilistic scoring rules.

8. Chuan Guo, Geoff Pleiss, Yu Sun, and Kilian Q. Weinberger. "On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks." ICML, 2017.
   - Used for: calibration and expected calibration error.

9. Tom Fawcett. "An Introduction to ROC Analysis." Pattern Recognition Letters, 2006.
   - Used for: AUROC.

10. Jesse Davis and Mark Goadrich. "The Relationship Between Precision-Recall and ROC Curves." ICML, 2006.
    - Used for: PR curves under rare-event settings.

11. Takaya Saito and Marc Rehmsmeier. "The Precision-Recall Plot Is More Informative than the ROC Plot When Evaluating Binary Classifiers on Imbalanced Datasets." PLOS ONE, 2015.
    - Used for: average precision / PR emphasis under class imbalance.
